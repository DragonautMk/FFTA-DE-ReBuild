    Song track sequences
    
  
    Explore one track or play the extracted tracks together. Known PCM and
  CGB wave instruments can use extracted samples; CGB square voices use their
  stored duty cycles. Fixed-rate PCM uses an assumed mixer rate that can be
  changed below; the default was observed in one European checkpoint. Other
  voices fall back to sine waves. Tempo commands from all tracks can guide an estimated playback
  rate, even when listening to one track. The manual rate applies before the
  first tempo command or when the option is disabled. Driver timing, bends,
  envelopes and effects remain approximate. The trace has safety limits.

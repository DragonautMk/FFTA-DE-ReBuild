Names come from actual CPU/Bus Serialize. Values are encoded as in saves; byte offsets shown here belong to the individual postcard field and are not guest addresses. Floating-point values are compared by IEEE bits.

Differing memory, pixel and stream indexes

Indexes in declared vectors, with at most 16 examples per entry. EWRAM/IWRAM/OAM are offsets in their respective buffers; pixels are RGB555 values, HLE values are f32 bits, FIFO entries are sample/period pairs.

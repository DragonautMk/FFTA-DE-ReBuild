Apply this preview

Apply rebuilds the plan under leases, rechecks every identity before the first removal and retains a receipt at each step. If anything changes, generate a new preview. Do not run commands from reports you do not own.

python3 scripts/debug_artifacts.py apply PATH/plan.json --receipt NEW_RECEIPT.json

An active or interrupted lease protects the session/cache. Leases are not removed automatically. Plan paths are bound to their current location; regenerate the preview after moving it.
